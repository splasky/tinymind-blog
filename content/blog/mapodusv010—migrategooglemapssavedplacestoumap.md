I just released **Mapodus v0.1.0**, my first open-source tool! 🎉

## What is Mapodus?

Mapodus is a desktop application that helps you migrate your **Google Maps Saved places** to [uMap](https://umap.openstreetmap.fr). If you've been saving locations on Google Maps over the years and want to move them to an open-source map platform, Mapodus makes it seamless.

## Features

- Export your Google Maps Saved lists
- Import directly into uMap
- Cross-platform (macOS, Windows, Linux)
